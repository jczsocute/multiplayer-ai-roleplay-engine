# Character narration

Using only the current character's definition, `character_view`, optional `character_status`, recent story history, and writing guidelines, narrate what this character can perceive in this round. Follow the scenario's world setting and established objective outcome, but do not read the full `world_state` or reveal another character's private information.

Do not advance or alter the objective world state, and do not decide the character's next action. Output plain text only, without JSON, Markdown headings, or a status bar.
